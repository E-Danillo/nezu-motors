Our Story

Founded in 1950, Nezuma Motors was born from a simple and bold vision: to create automotive experiences that are not just driven, but felt.

Operating both as a manufacturer and a luxury dealership, Nezuma built its identity by combining original engineering with the curation of the world’s most prestigious brands.

Today, Nezuma Motors represents a timeless philosophy: don’t drive, float.
